## Regulatory status (hard constraint — recurring relevance across any StockFox assignment)
- NOT a SEBI Registered Analyst (SRA) or Investment Adviser. Site explicitly directs users to registered professionals. No buy recommendations, no personalized investment advice permitted. [source: stockfox.pro | confidence 0.9 | last_verified 2026-08-07]
- Regulatory backdrop: SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations 2014, amended through Dec 2024 (FAQs July 2025) — "research services" explicitly includes buy/sell/hold recommendations, price targets, model portfolios, opinions on securities; RA registration required regardless of whether paid. SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations 2013 — personalized advice "for consideration" requires IA registration. 2023–2025 "finfluencer" rules bar unregistered creators/platforms from direct or indirect stock recommendations or implied return claims; educational content is expected to reference price data ≥3 months old. [source: sebi.gov.in regulation text + convergent secondary legal analysis (taxguru.in, moneylife.in, legal500.com, exchange4media.com) | confidence 0.7 | last_verified 2026-08-07]
- Genuinely open/unsettled: no SEBI guidance found that explicitly rules on whether an objectively-sorted, criteria-disclosed screener/ranking itself constitutes a "recommendation." Treat as inference territory in any future assignment, not settled law. [confidence 0.4 — flagged ambiguity, not a fact | last_verified 2026-08-07]
