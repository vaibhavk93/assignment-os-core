# Company Memory: Ixigo

_Last updated: 2026-07-05_

## Business Model
India's second-largest OTA (after MakeMyTrip with 60% market share). Revenue streams: (1) booking commissions (₹20-35 per rail booking, varying on bus/flight/cab), (2) advertising revenue, (3) high-margin ancillary services (ixigo Assured micro-insurance, Ixigo Money virtual currency). Key competitive advantage: customer acquisition through railway apps at low CAC, then cross-sold to higher-margin verticals. [confidence: high | source: FY26 earnings, GrowthX analysis]

## Products
**Domestic:** Trains (core), buses, flights, hotels. **International:** Flight search/booking, hotel booking, travel insurance, group bookings. **Recent launches:** ChatGPT native apps (June 2026) for conversational travel planning; Brevistay acquisition (June 2026) for hotel consolidation. **Features:** Price lock (4-14 days), smart web check-in, group booking (9+), real-time flight status. [confidence: high | source: App Store, ixigo.com, press releases]

## Target Market
Primary: Indian travelers. Strength in smaller cities (ease + speed positioning). Expanding into: European rail (Trenes.com acquisition Feb 2026), Middle East (Dubai focus implied). International outbound specifically for Indian travelers to Bangkok, Dubai, Singapore corridors (assignment context). [confidence: medium-high | source: Investor reports, recent acquisitions]

## Competitors
**MakeMyTrip:** 60% market share, dominant. **Cleartrip:** 7-9% share, air-focused, high CAC, simplicity positioning. **EaseMyTrip:** 7-9% share, cost-focused, no convenience fees. **Yatra:** 7-9% share. Ixigo positioning: low CAC, speed, AI integration, expanding to high-margin categories and international. [confidence: high | source: Market research reports, WhalesBook analysis]
