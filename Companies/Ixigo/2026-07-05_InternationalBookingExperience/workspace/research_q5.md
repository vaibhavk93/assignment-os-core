## OTA vs Direct Booking Shift

### Current State (2026)
- **OTA penetration**: 81.74% of online travel bookings in India
- **Online penetration overall**: 42.3% of all outbound travel transactions go online; 57.7% still booked offline
- **Direct booking share**: Growing at 9.39% CAGR but from lower base; estimated ~18% of online travel (inverse of OTA 81.74%)

### Shift Dynamics (2025-2026 trend)
- **OTAs pushing into international**: "India's OTA to see growth from international travel and non-air services" — domestic aviation growth slowing; international is strategic pivot for all major OTAs
- **Company confirmation**: Ixigo reporting international flight business expanding faster than domestic to investors
- **Mobile accelerating OTA dominance**: 65.37% of bookings now on mobile (apps), growing at 14.39% CAGR — favors OTA platforms with native apps over offline agents

### Forecast Direction
- OTA share likely to *increase* in international travel as:
  1. Mobile-first consumers (65%+ of volume) default to apps
  2. Visa/forex complexity creates demand for integrated booking platforms (OTA advantage)
  3. International flights less price-parity-driven than domestic; OTA value-add (recommendations, insurance, itinerary) matters more
- Direct airline share may hold in corporate/business travel; erodes in leisure/first-time travelers

## Gaps in This Research

1. **OTA share breakdown by corridor**: Research found national OTA share (81.74%) but not Bangkok/Dubai/Singapore–specific shares. Recommend assuming parity or slight variance based on local competitive intensity.

2. **GMV for international flights specifically**: Found overall outbound market (USD 23-34B), but not isolated international flight GMV. Likely significant portion (given flight is primary outbound transport), but exact figure not in budget.

3. **Offline travel agent share**: Found OTA dominance (81.74% online, 42.3% overall penetration), but not precise offline-agent-to-direct-airline ratio. Recommend treating "offline" as consolidated competitive set.

4. **Visa-on-arrival vs direct booking correlation**: Found that visa friction (especially Bangkok's recent ease) drives volume, but no data on whether visa-friction-reduction shifts consumers toward OTAs (more likely) or direct bookings.

5. **Prosus AI investment impact on international**: Ixigo has $36.5M in AI budget (from context.md), but no published data on how much is earmarked for international vs domestic. Recommend inferring from international-faster-than-domestic messaging.
