## Core Business Model

Ixigo operates as India's **second-largest technology-driven OTA** (after MakeMyTrip with 60% market share). The company aggregates and retails travel solutions across trains, flights, buses, and hotels. Revenue streams: (1) booking commissions on train/bus/flight/cab ticketing (₹20-35 per rail booking), (2) advertising revenue from brands, and (3) high-margin ancillary services like "ixigo Assured" (AI-driven micro-insurance for cancellation risk). Key competitive advantage: customer acquisition through its railway apps at fraction of competitor CAC, then cross-sold to higher-margin verticals (flights, buses, hotels). [confidence: 0.9 | source: Q2 FY26 earnings; GrowthX business model analysis]

[confidence: 0.85 | source: Market research]

**Primary Competitors & Positioning:**

| Competitor | Key Differentiator | Focus Area |
|---|---|---|
| **MakeMyTrip** | Market leader; 2017 Ibibo merger; extensive corporate partnerships | Flights, hotels, packages |
| **Cleartrip** | Simplicity & reliability; high CAC model; airline integrations | Air travel focus; expanding to hotels/intl |
| **EaseMyTrip** | Cost-focused; no convenience fees; competitive domestic fares | Budget travelers; affordability positioning |
| **Ixigo** | Speed + simplicity; strong in smaller cities; AI integration; low-CAC (rail base) | Domestic rail as moat; now expanding intl + AI |

**Ixigo's Strategic Positioning:** Both Ixigo and Cleartrip are embracing AI to transform from intermediaries to orchestrators of entire travel journey. Ixigo differentiates on low customer acquisition cost (leveraging its railway app dominance) and is now expanding aggressively into high-margin categories (hotels, international, AI-driven personalization).
