## The Real Question
What is the AI-native product architecture that makes international travel on ixigo feel as effortless as booking a domestic train ticket — and is this the right bet for ixigo to make right now?

## Core Objective
Design a coherent, AI-native end-to-end international travel experience for Indian customers on ixigo — covering the full journey from destination discovery through post-booking travel phase — with AI embedded as the orchestration layer (not a UI element), backed by a credible business case and phased rollout plan for a public Indian OTA.

## Success Criteria

| # | Criterion | Testable Check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI-native solution design — NOT a chatbot | No slide/section presents a chat interface as the primary solution; AI manifests as personalization, proactive guidance, contextual intelligence |
| 2 | Full journey coverage | Submission addresses all 5 stages: discovery → research/info → booking → pre-travel → during travel |
| 3 | Indian customer specificity | At least 3 India-specific pain points addressed with India-specific solutions (visa, forex/UPI, first-time intl traveler anxiety) |
| 4 | Algorithm/personalization layer specified | At least one concrete algorithm or ranking change described (not just UX change) |
| 5 | Business case answered | "Should ixigo prioritize this?" answered with explicit reasoning tied to GMV, competitive moat, or market timing |
| 6 | Metrics defined | Success metrics are specific, measurable, and cover conversion, satisfaction, AND business impact — not just vanity |
| 7 | Monitoring framework | Ongoing health monitoring described with specific signals/dashboards — not just "we'll track NPS" |
| 8 | Rollout strategy | Phased rollout with clear rationale — not just "phase 1 then phase 2" |
| 9 | Funnel changes specified | Explicit call-out of what changes in flight/hotel booking funnel for international vs domestic |

## Recommendation Frame
A case study structured as: **Problem diagnosis (India-specific) → AI-native solution vision → Journey-by-stage product design → Algorithm layer → Business case → Metrics + monitoring → Rollout**

The recommendation is NOT "build these 10 features." It is: "Here is a new product paradigm for international travel on ixigo — this is the AI-native architecture, here is why it works, here is why ixigo should bet on it now."

## Scope

**In scope:**
- India outbound international travel (primary) — Bangkok, Dubai, Singapore corridor initially
- Discovery through post-booking experience
- AI/ML personalization and ranking
- Booking funnel modifications
- Payment/forex simplification
- Visa information layer
- Launch metrics, health monitoring, rollout phasing

**Deprioritize:**
- Inbound international travel to India (not asked)
- B2B/corporate travel (consumer focus)
- Deep technical architecture (PM-level, not engineering spec)

**Out of scope:**
- Domestic travel (already works)
- Hotel experience redesign as primary focus (flights are the anchor)
