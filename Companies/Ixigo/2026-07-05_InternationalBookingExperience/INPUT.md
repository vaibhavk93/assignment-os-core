**Product Management Case Study — International Booking Experience**

Today, ixigo helps consumers book flight tickets, hotels, and plan travel itineraries. We help our customers finalize their travel plans by inspiring them with trending itineraries, book flight tickets, and hotels at the best possible prices with features like fare outlook and price lock, and simplify rescheduling and cancellations for unforeseen scenarios with the help of ixigo Assure and ixigo Flex.

**Problems and opportunities:**
Indian customers often face challenges when booking international flights. These challenges include:
- Discovery is broken. The pain of exploring and finding the right destination.
- Uncertainties about visa requirements, baggage allowances, and in-flight amenities.
- The complexity of Booking Process: Multiple steps, options, and cancellations/rescheduling terms can be overwhelming
- Difficulty in customizing flight options based on specific preferences
- Complications in processing international payments and understanding currency conversions

**Solutions:**
- Think about the user experience at ixigo for international travel planning. How should we make this experience discoverable, simple, and comprehensive for customers?
- Think about the various stages of planning an international holiday/vacation. How should the customer experience evolve from the planning to the travel phase?
- What experience and algorithm changes will be needed to make this work? Should we make any changes to the flight and hotel booking funnel to cater to international travel bookings?
- How do all the changes you've suggested come together to deliver an end-to-end new experience for our customers?
- How would you solve or build customer experience for international flyers with an AI-First and AI-Native mindset?

**Launch:**
- Should ixigo make solving this a priority? Why / why not?
- If launched, how would you measure the success of this product for ixigo?
- If launched, how would you monitor the ongoing health of this product?
- What would your rollout strategy be and why?

**Case Study Objectives:**
- AI First and AI Native mindset and approach
- Identify key pain points faced by Indian customers when planning international travel.
- Propose innovative solutions to simplify the planning and booking process and enhance user experience.
- Evaluate the impact of proposed solutions on customer satisfaction, conversion rates, and overall business growth.
- The proposed solution must not be a plain vanilla AI chatbot.
