# Writing Style — Personal Voice Reference

Personal voice and style preferences. Applied by Case Builder and Formatter agents when drafting output.

**NOTE: This file needs to be filled in by the user. Default values below are reasonable starting points.**

---

## Voice Profile

**Tone:** Direct, confident, slightly informal. Not corporate. Not overly casual.

**Perspective:** First-person where appropriate ("I recommend..."). Avoid passive voice.

**Sentence length:** Prefer short. Mix in longer sentences for nuance. Never three long sentences in a row.

**Paragraph length:** 3-4 sentences max in memos/documents. Bullets preferred for lists of 3+.

---

## Vocabulary Preferences

**Prefer these words:**
- "because" (not "due to the fact that")
- "use" (not "utilize")
- "show" (not "demonstrate")
- "key" (not "critical" or "crucial" everywhere)
- "drive" (for metrics / business outcomes)
- "north star" (for primary metrics)

**Avoid these:**
- "leverage" (overused)
- "synergy" / "ecosystem" / "holistic" (jargon)
- "very", "really", "quite" (weak qualifiers)
- "I feel" (use "I think" or "data suggests")
- "Obviously" / "Clearly" (condescending)
- "Moving forward" (corporate filler)
- "At the end of the day" (cliché)

---

## Sentence Openers

**Good openers:**
- Lead with the finding: "Retention dropped 12% in Q3..."
- Lead with the implication: "This matters because..."
- Lead with the recommendation: "The highest-leverage fix is..."

**Avoid openers:**
- "It is important to note that..." (just say it)
- "In this analysis, we will..." (just analyze it)
- "It can be argued that..." (argue it)

---

## Numbers and Data

**Format:**
- Use commas: 1,000,000 not 1000000
- Use % not "percent" in bullets
- Round to 2 significant figures unless precision matters: "~15%" not "14.87%"
- Use "~" prefix for estimates: "~$50M TAM"
- Always label currency: "$50M" not "50M"

**State uncertainty explicitly:**
- "Based on app store reviews (n=~200 recent reviews)..."
- "Industry benchmark: D30 retention ~25-35% for travel apps (Redseer 2023)"

---

## Recommendation Format

**For a single recommendation:**
> [Recommendation]. [Reason — 1 sentence]. [Expected impact — quantified or estimated]. [Risk or tradeoff — 1 sentence].

**For multiple recommendations:**
> Prioritize by impact × confidence × effort. Always rank. Never present as a flat list.

---

## What to Avoid in Output

- Do NOT write "In conclusion" sections — end with the next step or clearest recommendation
- Do NOT hedge everything with "potentially" and "may" — own the recommendation
- Do NOT add a "disclaimer" unless legally required
- Do NOT summarize the summary — the exec summary IS the summary, don't repeat it
